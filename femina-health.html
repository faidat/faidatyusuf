THE CHALLENGE & APPROACH

One service. One relevant next step.

Femina Health wanted to encourage women to book appointments for naturopathic treatment across several service areas. I worked to make the path from search intent to discovery call more relevant and measurable.

01

Research

Researched keywords and audiences, then developed personas to guide campaign and page decisions.

02

Segment

Created service-led campaigns for areas including hormone health, gut health and fertility.

03

Align

Created dedicated landing pages with service-specific messaging and a discovery-call CTA.

04

Measure

Integrated Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity; used behavioural insights and heatmaps to identify optimization opportunities.
